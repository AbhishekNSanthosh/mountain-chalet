Located in Times Square, CozyStay apartment hotel provide a peaceful, private retreat in the heart of one of the world's most iconic cities.

Our history of hospitality and fine living began decades ago, when we first envisioned a space that transcends the regular hotel experience. CozyStay was born from a passion for exquisite interiors, modern kitchen equipment, and personal service that makes you feel at home.
