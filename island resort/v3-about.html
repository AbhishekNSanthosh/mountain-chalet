Welcome to CozyStay Pacific Hotel. Nestled in the heart of the Pacific Islands resort, on the edge of a tranquil and beautiful Garden Island, CozyStay is a haven of warmth, tranquility and rejuvenation. Each villa and suite has been thoughtfully designed to blend effortlessly with the natural surroundings.

Bathed in golden Pacific light, surrounded by lush tropical gardens and the gentle sound of waves, our resort offers an unparalleled escape from the world. From the moment you arrive, you will be immersed in warmth, beauty and serenity that defines our distinctive island lifestyle.

Our Heritage Â· Since 1994

Her Lecuring Journey

Our resort was born from a dream — a vision of a place where nature and luxury existed in perfect harmony. Founded in 1994 by our pioneering founders, the resort has grown from a small collection of beach huts into one of the South Pacific's most celebrated destinations.

Over the decades, we have carefully cultivated our relationships with the local communities and the natural environment. Every structure, every garden path, every sunset terrace has been placed with intention and care. The journey continues every day, shaped by the stories of those who visit and the staff who call this island home.

Today the resort encompasses twelve private villas, twenty overwater bungalows, six restaurants and bars, a world-class spa, and a dedicated marine conservation program — all without ever losing sight of the original dream.

A World of Choice and Endless Adventure Await You

From the thrill of deep-sea diving in crystal-clear lagoons to the quiet pleasure of watching the sunrise from your private terrace, CozyStay offers an extraordinary range of experiences for every traveller.

Our activity programme encompasses watersports, cultural excursions, guided jungle treks, sunset sailing, and much more. Expert guides and instructors are on hand to ensure every activity is safe, memorable and perfectly tailored to your interests.

Whether you seek adventure or absolute stillness, the South Pacific is waiting for you. The only challenge you will face is deciding what to do first.
