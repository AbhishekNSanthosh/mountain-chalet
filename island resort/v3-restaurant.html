Fine Dining / South Pacific

Indulge Your Senses and
Discover New Flavors

From sunrise to sunset, our restaurant has long been part of the CozyStay experience. Our menus display the very best in culinary tradition, brought to life by dedicated chefs who celebrate local produce, artful technique and inspired flavour combinations.

This gastronomic haven has been around for 20 years, serving the finest, most delicious and most creative cuisine to resort guests and outside visitors alike. Our menu prides itself on being an ever-evolving, rotating menu using the freshest locally-sourced and seasonal ingredients to craft unforgettable dining moments. Reservations are recommended.
