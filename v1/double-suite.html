Double Suite

Romantic Alpine Retreat

Suite Overview

A Sanctuary Made for Two

Perched on the fourth floor of CozyStay Resort, the Double Suite is the definitive romantic escape — a 60 square metre haven meticulously designed for couples who demand the very best. The heart of the suite is a magnificent king-size bed swathed in handpicked natural linens, positioned to face a private fireplace whose warm amber glow transforms cold alpine evenings into something almost magical. Beyond the bedroom, a generously proportioned sitting area furnished with velvet armchairs and a bespoke coffee table creates the perfect setting for quiet evenings in, complete with a chilled bottle of our welcome champagne.

The marble bathroom is a sanctuary in itself: a freestanding soaking tub filled with mountain-mineral bath salts invites long, languid soaks, while panoramic windows reveal a sweeping view of the ancient forest canopy below — still, serene, and utterly private. The heated marble floors, Aesop amenities, and plush robes ensure that every moment spent here feels indulgent. A 4K smart television concealed within an antique walnut cabinet, together with a Nespresso machine and a curated in-room drinks cabinet, means you have everything you need without ever leaving your suite.

As guests of the Double Suite, you have exclusive access to CozyStay's couples spa — a private thermal pool and treatment suite reserved just for you — as well as a dedicated butler who can arrange everything from candlelit terrace dinners to helicopter excursions over the peaks. Whether celebrating an anniversary, a honeymoon, or simply the pleasure of each other's company, the Double Suite offers an experience that words can only begin to capture.
