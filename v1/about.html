About CozyStay

Our Story & Heritage

Our Story

A Vision Born in the Alps

CozyStay Resort was founded in 1998 by Hans and Elise Müller, a husband-and-wife duo who fell in love with the Swiss Alps on their honeymoon and never quite found their way back down. What began as a modest eight-room chalet perched above Zermatt has grown, over nearly three decades, into one of the region's most celebrated boutique mountain retreats — without ever losing the intimate warmth and personal attention that defined it from the very first night a guest walked through its doors.

Hans's background in sustainable architecture and Elise's lifelong passion for alpine gastronomy shaped CozyStay's founding philosophy: that true luxury is inseparable from the landscape that surrounds it. Every structural decision, every seasonal menu, every carefully curated local experience has been guided by a deep respect for the mountains, the forests, and the communities that call this corner of Switzerland home. Guests often describe CozyStay as feeling less like a hotel and more like a home — one that simply happens to have extraordinarily attentive staff and breathtaking views in every direction.

Today, with Hans and Elise still actively involved in the resort's direction and their daughter, Anna, leading the next generation of leadership, CozyStay continues to evolve while holding firmly to its founding values. New suites, expanded wellness facilities, and a thriving culinary programme sit alongside a commitment to carbon neutrality and community partnerships that reflect the Müllers' belief that looking after people and looking after the planet are never in contradiction.

Our Philosophy

What We Stand For

Alpine Hospitality

We believe hospitality is an art form rooted in genuine warmth. Every guest is welcomed as a cherished friend, and every interaction is an opportunity to surprise and delight. Our team are ambassadors not just for CozyStay, but for the alpine way of life — generous, unhurried, and deeply human.

Sustainable Luxury

True luxury leaves the world better than it found it. CozyStay operates with a carbon-neutral commitment, sources produce from local alpine farms, and works with regional craftspeople and artisans. We believe that caring for the environment and offering a five-star experience are not in competition — they are inseparable.

Unforgettable Memories

We measure success not in occupancy rates but in the stories our guests take home with them. The first ski lesson, the candlelit dinner under a full alpine moon, the morning when the mist parted to reveal a view that stole the breath — these are the moments we dedicate ourselves to creating, day after day, season after season.

Our People

The People Behind CozyStay

Marcus Huber

General Manager

With over 18 years of luxury hospitality experience across Switzerland and Austria, Marcus joined CozyStay in 2014. His calm authority and encyclopaedic knowledge of the Alps — he once guided a solo traverse of four cantons — have made him the heart of every guest's alpine adventure.

Sofia Berger

Head Chef

Trained in Lausanne and refined in the kitchens of Lyon and Copenhagen, Sofia returned to Switzerland with a singular mission: to celebrate the extraordinary larder of the Swiss Alps. Her farm-to-table menus change with the seasons and her cheese fondue is, by all accounts, the finest in the canton.

Lena Kessler

Wellness Director

A certified physiotherapist and certified yoga instructor with training in Balinese and Ayurvedic traditions, Lena oversees CozyStay's entire wellness programme. Her morning yoga sessions on the terrace — mist rising from the valley below — have become a beloved ritual for returning guests from around the world.
