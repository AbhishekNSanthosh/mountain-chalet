SPORTS & ADVENTURE

Winter Activities

CozyStay Andermatt offers a wide range of winter sports. The modern ski resort is known for its many slopes, gentle descents, long steep runs and deep powder descents. You'll also find trails for winter hiking, snowshoeing and cross-country skiing.
