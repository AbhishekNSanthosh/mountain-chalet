Hotel Rates, Information & Reservation Policies

Napa Valley is the heart of wine country. Staying with us in the Napa Mill neighborhood means you're just steps from some of the best wineries and restaurants in the world.

  • Rates exclude taxes and daily hospitality fee which covers all suite amenities.
  • A guest must be at least 21 years of age to book a suite and check-in at the lodge.
  • A valid credit card is required to secure every suite reservation.
  • Strict 7-day cancellation policy applies during high season; please check your specific booking dates for details.
  • Pets are not allowed in the suites.
  • Smoking is strictly prohibited in all indoor areas.
  • Check-in is from 4pm; Check-out is before 11am.
  • Extra adult charges apply for more than two guests per suite.
