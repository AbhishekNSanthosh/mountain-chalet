Our Services

Exceptional Amenities for Every Guest

Our Promise

Every Detail, Considered

At CozyStay, service is not a department - it is a disposition. From the moment you arrive, every member of our team is engaged in a single, quietly ambitious project: making your time here the finest of your year. We offer a comprehensive range of services not as a checklist, but as an expression of genuine care for your comfort, your adventure, and your wellbeing.

Whether you wish to spend your days carving powder on pristine pistes, soaking in thermal pools above the treeline, or simply reading by the fire with a glass of Swiss Pinot Noir and no demands on your time whatsoever, CozyStay's team is here to make it effortlessly possible.

Fine Dining

Farm-to-table alpine cuisine crafted from the finest seasonal Swiss produce. Our restaurant celebrates the flavours of the mountains, changing its menu with each season and each morning's harvest.

Spa & Wellness

Restorative treatments, thermal pools, and healing rituals drawn from both Swiss and Eastern traditions. Our spa is a world of calm designed to leave you profoundly renewed.

Ski Concierge

Premium equipment rental, lift pass procurement, private tuition with certified instructors, and insider knowledge of the best runs - so you spend less time queuing and more time on the snow.

Airport Transfer

Arrive in style with our luxury vehicle pickup service from Geneva, Zurich, or Milan airports. Your CozyStay experience begins the moment you step off the plane.

Hiking Guides

Expert local mountain guides who know every trail, every hidden viewpoint, and every secret waterfall in the surrounding peaks. Let us take you off the beaten path and into the wild heart of the Alps.

Yoga & Meditation

Morning yoga and guided meditation sessions led by our resident instructor on the terrace, with the mountains as your backdrop. A deeply restorative start to any alpine day.

Wine Cellar Tours

Curated Swiss wine tastings in CozyStay's private cellar, guided by our sommelier. Discover the extraordinary Pinot Noirs, Chasselas whites, and rare alpine varieties that few visitors ever encounter.

Childcare Services

Supervised and engaging kids' programmes run by qualified childcare professionals, giving parents the rare gift of uninterrupted relaxation while the little ones go on their own alpine adventures.

Business Lounge

State-of-the-art conference and meeting facilities for up to 30 delegates, with high-speed connectivity, A/V equipment, and full catering. Because inspiration doesn't pause at altitude.

Laundry & Valet

A 24-hour laundry and pressing service ensures your wardrobe is always immaculate, whether you are heading to a formal dinner or back onto the mountain for another day of adventure.

Cycling Tours

Premium e-bike rentals and guided cycling routes through alpine meadows, lakeside paths, and forest trails. Our guides tailor every route to your pace, your ambition, and your appetite for discovery.

Room Service

24-hour gourmet in-room dining, drawing from the full depth of our kitchen's repertoire. From a pre-dawn espresso before an early summit attempt to a midnight fondue for two - we are always here.

A Culinary Destination

Signature Alpine Dining

CozyStay's restaurant is recognised as one of the finest dining destinations in the Swiss Alps - a place where the landscape outside the window and the landscape on the plate tell the same story. Head Chef Sofia Berger sources every ingredient with the rigour of a curator and the passion of someone who grew up foraging in these very forests.

The seasonal tasting menu evolves with the mountains: wild ramp risotto and morel broth in spring; trout tartare with alpine herbs and glacier water pearls in summer; venison slow-braised with root vegetables and mountain juniper in autumn; and a warming celebration of cheese, charcuterie, and heritage grains through the long winter months.

Our wine list, curated by in-house sommelier Lukas Brandt, features over 200 Swiss labels alongside a thoughtful international selection. Private dining in the wine cellar can be arranged for groups of up to twelve, complete with a personalised tasting flight and chef's table experience.
